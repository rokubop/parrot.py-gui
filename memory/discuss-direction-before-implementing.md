---
name: discuss-direction-before-implementing
description: Lay out diagnosis and options first, then execute the agreed scope without stacking further questions
type: feedback
---

For anything with a design dimension, open with what is actually wrong today -
quoting real strings and `file:line` - then options with a recommendation and the
trade-off named. Offer a choice only where the answer would change the work.

Once a direction is set, build the whole thing and report what was and was not
verified. Further questions are drag; judgement calls are fine to mention
afterwards.
